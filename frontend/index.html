Unified Risk Scoring & Decision Pipeline

1. Unified Risk Score (0–100): Fused composite index synthesizing machine-learning probability and static contextual indicators.

• Learned Fusion: Unified Risk = 100 × P_fusion, where P_fusion = σ(w^T x + b)
• Fallback Baseline: Unified Risk = 100 × (w_model · P_model + w_context · C_context) / (w_model + w_context) [w_model = 0.85, w_context = 0.15]

2. Prediction Likelihood (%): The model's statistical class confidence for the predicted class. For threat classifications, it displays P(Threat) × 100; for legitimate classifications, it displays P(Legitimate) = [1 − P(Threat)] × 100.

3. Contextual Risk Score (0–100): Independent, deterministic score evaluating static structural & environmental red flags (e.g. numeric IP hosts, anomalous sections, urgency lexicons, or executable download extensions).

4. Explainable AI (SHAP TreeExplainer): Exact Shapley attributions satisfying f(x) = φ_0 + Σ φ_i. Positive values (+φ_i) elevate risk toward the threat class; negative values (-φ_i) mitigate risk. Bar widths reflect relative absolute impact (|φ_i| / max|φ| × 100).

0–29: LOW (Allow)
30–59: MEDIUM (Caution)
60–79: HIGH (Warn)
80–100: CRITICAL (Block/Quarantine)
